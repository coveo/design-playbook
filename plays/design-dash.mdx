## When to use it

- You have a framed problem and need breadth of ideas quickly.
- You want engineers and PMs sketching alongside designers.

## How to run it

1. Recap the framing statement and constraints.
2. Individual sketching rounds (crazy-8s or similar).
3. Share back, cluster, and vote.
4. Converge on 1–2 directions with named owners.

## Outputs

- Ranked solution directions.
- Owners and next steps for each direction.

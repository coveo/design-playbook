Coming soon — the habit, not the process: when a designer spots a misaligned component, a stale pattern, or a UX paper cut, they own it — flag it, fix it if it's small, or drive it to whoever can. The craft of noticing, the judgment of when to just fix it, and the follow-through that stops "someone should fix that" from being the end of the sentence.

## When to use it

- You have raw research or discovery material and no shared framing yet.
- The team is jumping to solutions before agreeing on the problem.

## How to run it

1. Collect raw observations from research, support tickets, and stakeholder input.
2. Sort each observation into **P**roblems, **O**pportunities, **I**nsights, **N**eeds, or **T**hemes.
3. Cluster within each column and name the clusters.
4. Dot-vote on the clusters that matter most and turn them into framing statements.

## Outputs

- A shared POINT board.
- 2–3 framing statements to carry into solution work.

## When to use it

- You have a prototype or shipped surface and an open product question.
- Before committing engineering effort to a risky direction.

## How to run it

1. Write goals and a discussion guide.
2. Recruit 5–8 participants that match the target segment.
3. Run sessions; record and take structured notes.
4. Synthesize with the **research synthesis** agent skill — findings get evidence and confidence labels.
5. Turn the synthesis into a shareable story with the **research narrative** skill.

## Outputs

- Evidence-backed findings with confidence labels.
- A readout deck or Confluence page for the team.

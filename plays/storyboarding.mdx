## When?

**After a Design Smash or provocations research.** The goal of the session is to align on what the user journey should look like and what we need to show in each step of the prototype.

## Step by step

### 1. Collate sketches/provocations

Bring the top-voted sketch from <PlayRef slug="design-smash" /> or all provocation concepts that were tested into a new file for the group. Agree on the direction or what's needed for this idea to be put in front of users. *Tip: it's usually just a few extra screens added before and after for it to work.*

### 2. Decide on the primary goal of the prototype

Talk about what you want to learn most by validating this idea. What is the primary goal? Are there secondary goals — are you testing multiple approaches, does the prototype have multiple user flows?

### 3. Write down the names of each step

Grab 6–7 post-its and treat them as the names of the screens. Your most-voted idea may go somewhere in the middle, but you may also want to deconstruct or expand it. *Tip: feeling stuck? Start with the first and last steps of the user journey.*

### 4. Flesh out what's going into the prototype

Starting with the first step, list everything you might want to show, then do this for all screens. Once you've listed all ideas, talk through them and decide which ones go into the prototype and which aren't important enough.

## What next?

**Prototyping.** Happy with the result? Time to turn the storyboard into an interactive prototype. Too many things to test, or too different from what users experience now?

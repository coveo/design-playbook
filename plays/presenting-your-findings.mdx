## When?

**After a synthesis, before the decisions.** You've run a round of calls and synthesized the findings with <PlayRef slug="synthesising-research" />. This play is the last mile: getting what you learned into the heads of the people who will act on it — the team, PMs, engineering leads, stakeholders.

## Why?

**Findings don't change roadmaps; stories do.** A synthesis document is evidence, but evidence alone gets archived. The teams that act on research are the ones who *felt* the user's problem in the readout. Presenting is a design task like any other: know your audience, structure the narrative, and make the evidence do the talking.

## Step by step

### 1. Know the audience and their decision

Who is in the room, and what decision should they be able to make when you're done? A dev team needs different bits than a VP. Write that decision down first — it's your narrative's job description.

### 2. Pick the best bits

You can't present everything, and trying kills the story. Choose the findings that bear on the decision: the surprises, the contradictions, and the strongest evidence. Park the rest in the appendix.

### 3. Build the arc

Context (what we asked and why) → tension (what we expected vs what we saw) → findings with evidence → so-what (the bets or recommendations). Lead every finding with the customer's own words — quotes connect where summaries don't.

### 4. Choose the format

A live readout with slides for decisions that need discussion; an executive summary for leadership; a Confluence page as the durable record. Often you need two of the three — generate them from the same synthesis rather than writing twice.

### 5. Present outcome-first

Open with the one-sentence takeaway, not the methodology. If people remember one thing, make sure you chose which thing.

## What next?

The findings feed the loop: strong evidence for a direction goes into a <PlayRef slug="shaping-workshop" /> or straight into prototyping; open questions become the research questions for your next round — start again at <PlayRef slug="creating-research-plan" />.

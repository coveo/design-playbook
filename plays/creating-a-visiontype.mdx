Coming soon — the north-star artifact: a deliberately ambitious prototype of a future moment, used to define direction and rally product, design, and engineering around it. Unlike <PlayRef slug="provocations" /> (which test assumptions) or a test prototype (which validates a solution), a visiontype's job is alignment — it should provoke "that's where we're going" rather than "does this work?".
